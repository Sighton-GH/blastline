# Status

User-approved source art: Batches 1–10.

Integration branch: `main`.

The approved PNG master sheets are present under `assets/source/blastline/` with stable numbered names. Optimized runtime crops are present under `assets/blastline/` and are reproducibly generated by `tools/extract-production-assets.py`.

The full master sheets are never requested by the browser runtime. The Canvas renderer continues to construct the flat roadway geometry, ocean base, bridge structure/cables, gates, projectiles, particles, and dynamic text at runtime. Optimized crops now provide tower detail, asphalt/ocean surface detail, and four-frame marching cycles for every standard enemy class.
